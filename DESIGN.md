# Design System: Premium Portfolio

## Visual Direction
Inspired by Vercel, Linear, and Raycast. Dark mode by default, high-contrast typography, and subtle spatial effects.

## Colors
- **Strategy**: Committed (One saturated accent role carrying 30-60% of brand identity).
- **Background**: Tinted Neutral (OKLCH L=15% C=0.01 H=250)
- **Primary**: Deep Blue / Cyan Accent (OKLCH L=60% C=0.15 H=240)
- **Neutral**: Tinted grays for hierarchy.

## Typography
- **Primary**: Sans-serif (Geist or Inter preferred)
- **Hierarchy**: Large, bold headings with generous letter-spacing (negative for large titles).

## Layout
- **Grid**: Discreete background grid for a "technical" feel.
- **Rhythm**: Varied spacing to avoid monotony.

## Effects
- **Glow**: Subtle radial gradients for depth.
- **Blur**: Glassmorphism used sparingly for overlays and navbars.
- **Motion**: Exponential ease-out curves. No bounce.

## Components (shadcn/ui + Framer Motion)
- **Cards**: Minimal borders, subtle hover glow.
- **Buttons**: Clear hierarchy, smooth transitions.
- **Timeline**: Modern dashboard style for experience.
